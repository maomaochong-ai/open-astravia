# Selection and stage gates

## Concept gate

Compare candidates against the same brief. Select for strategic fit, subject/product fidelity, composition, extensibility across formats, and production risk. Novelty alone is not enough.

Record:

- selected node and why;
- rejected alternatives and the decisive tradeoff;
- invariants carried into production;
- risks to validate in the next proof.

## Still/master gate

Require acceptable identity or product geometry, palette, light direction, copy space, crop resilience, and absence of severe artifacts. The master becomes the authority for downstream references.

## Motion gate

Validate a short representative shot for reference adherence, motion coherence, camera control, final state, and audio intent. Fix systematic problems before rendering a sequence.

## Delivery gate

Check every declared deliverable exists and matches its source node, channel, ratio, duration/resolution, safe areas, continuity rules, and intended ordering. Verify rendered content rather than relying on successful job status.

## Stale-work rule

When an approved upstream asset or core brief changes, identify downstream nodes whose assumptions no longer hold. Re-review or regenerate only those affected artifacts. Keep unaffected outputs.
