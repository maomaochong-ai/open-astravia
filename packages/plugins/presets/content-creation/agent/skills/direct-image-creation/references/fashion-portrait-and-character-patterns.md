# Fashion, portrait, and character patterns

## Portrait direction

Define identity, expression as physical cues, pose, gaze, crop, environment, key/fill/rim, skin texture, wardrobe material, palette, and retouching limit. Mixed practical light must state what each source does to the face and clothing.

Useful portrait mechanisms:

- backlit street: preserve face exposure, rim hair/wardrobe, environment carries depth;
- mixed practical night: two motivated color sources, controlled skin exposure;
- high-contrast profile: silhouette and one accent artifact;
- analog memory: lifted or compressed tonal behavior plus a specific environmental cue;
- underwater/surreal: consistent buoyancy, hair/fabric drift, caustic light, visible face state.

## Fashion systems

- Triptych: hero silhouette, fabric/detail, movement/action.
- Editorial grid: same person/wardrobe with assigned angles.
- Type-led poster: exact copy, figure/type depth relationship, protected legibility.
- Lookbook: consistent background/light, one changing garment or pose axis.
- Techwear/sports: garment construction and body action remain the focal evidence; abstract forms support depth.

## Character production

Create an identity ledger and approve a neutral anchor before variants.

- Turnaround: front, three-quarter/profile, back; aligned baseline and proportions.
- Expression sheet: fixed angle/light/hair, only facial action changes.
- Outfit grid: fixed identity/body/pose, only wardrobe and accessories change.
- Reference card: full body, portrait, signature items, palette; all views agree.
- Stylized/chibi variant: preserve distinguishing markers and palette while explicitly changing proportions/material.

Generate one asset at a time when identity matters more than overview speed. Labels and multi-cell text require a text-capable profile and delivery-size review.

## Quality gate

Check bone structure, age cues, hairline, skin tone, body proportions, garment continuity, material drape, hands, accessories, and whether movement changes identity. Do not approve a pack from averages; reject any asset that breaks the authority ledger.
