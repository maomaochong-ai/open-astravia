# Image task patterns

## Product hero

1. Define product geometry and brand invariants.
2. Choose one composition and lighting concept per candidate.
3. Generate low-cost candidates.
4. Select on silhouette, material fidelity, label/logo stability, and copy space.
5. Produce the hero, then derive formats from the approved master.

## Character or photo pack

1. Establish an identity/reference sheet and continuity ledger.
2. Approve a neutral anchor image.
3. Branch into poses, expressions, wardrobe, or environments one axis at a time.
4. Reject identity drift before making downstream assets.

## Storyboard or video keyframes

1. Write shot jobs and continuity anchors.
2. Generate composition-first frames; prioritize staging and screen direction over final polish.
3. Approve start/end states before connecting frames to video nodes.
4. Keep character, product, environment, and lens language consistent across shots.

## Social asset set

1. Define a master visual system: palette, typography area, subject scale, and brand rules.
2. Produce one approved master.
3. Create format-specific compositions rather than blind crops when hierarchy changes.
4. Validate safe zones and legibility at delivery size.

## Ad creative variants

Create candidates around distinct hypotheses: benefit demonstration, product beauty, social proof, problem/solution, or lifestyle context. Keep brand invariants fixed and record the hypothesis in each node purpose.
