# Video quality checklist

Evaluate against the workflow objective, not against generic cinematic taste.

## Creative fidelity

- The subject, product, action, environment, and intended ending are present.
- Camera motion and pacing match the requested energy.
- Composition and aspect ratio suit the publishing surface.
- Style, palette, lighting, and continuity anchors remain consistent.

## Temporal quality

- Motion begins and ends intentionally.
- Subject and camera motion do not conflict without purpose.
- Transformations have understandable intermediate states.
- The shot does not contain more narrative beats than its duration can support.

## Artifact review

- identity, faces, hands, text, logos, and product geometry remain stable;
- objects do not merge, pop, stretch, or disappear unexpectedly;
- camera movement avoids unintended jitter or speed changes;
- loops or ending frames do not jump unless intentional;
- audio, when generated, matches visible timing and contains no unwanted speech.

## Iteration discipline

Change one major variable at a time. Prefer editing motion language before replacing a strong reference image. Replace the reference when its artifacts, composition, or ambiguity are the likely cause. Regenerate only after explaining which criterion failed and what the next attempt changes.
