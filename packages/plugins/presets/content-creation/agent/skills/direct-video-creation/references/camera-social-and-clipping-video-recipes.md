# Camera, social, and clipping video recipes

## Drone or aerial shot

Choose a path and destination:

- reveal: begin occluded/low, ascend or clear foreground, resolve on the subject;
- orbit: define radius, direction, subject lock, and final angle;
- flyover: define altitude/speed, terrain path, parallax, and arrival point;
- top-down: keep optical axis stable and give ground motion a clear pattern.

Specify stabilization, lens/field of view, time/weather, wind/ambience, and the final framing. "Drone shot" without a destination creates aimless motion.

## One-shot or continuous take

Define one traversable path through connected space. Use entrances, foreground wipes, doorways, subject turns, or focus changes as internal punctuation without hidden cuts unless requested. State "continuous, no cuts" and prohibit contradictory camera moves. End at a deliberate destination or reveal.

## FPV or architectural flythrough

FPV requires continuous forward movement and nearby depth cues. Architectural flythroughs require physically connected rooms/passages, consistent light, and plausible camera clearance. If the route cannot be drawn as a simple path, split it into shots.

## Brand-aware social video

1. Inspect available brand identity, audience/ICP, messaging, supplied assets, and current campaign objective. Note missing sources rather than inventing them.
2. Write platform copy: hook, compact body/proof, CTA. Keep claims verified.
3. Build a duration-sized storyboard with an immediate opening hook and a readable final brand state.
4. Convert it to a director brief using platform ratio/safe zones, camera, motivated light, audio, and timed beats.
5. Generate only the necessary reference frames: product/character authority, start/end frames, or one style/environment anchor.
6. Review on the destination surface, including UI-safe areas and the first second.

Platform behavior and current publishing specs can change; treat inspected/project requirements as authority instead of embedding a permanent table.

## Long-form video to short clips

Current Vetta can plan and arrange timeline clips, but transcription/highlight ranking or automatic face tracking may require capabilities not exposed by the content workflow. Inspect before execution.

When supported, use this pipeline:

1. Ingest and transcribe with timestamps and speaker boundaries.
2. Generate candidate spans around complete thoughts, preserving setup/payoff.
3. Score candidates on hook immediacy, emotional peak, strong opinion, revelation, conflict, quotability, story climax, and practical value.
4. Reject duplicates/overlapping candidates; do not pad the requested count with weak clips.
5. For each survivor record title, start/end, hook line, score/rationale, speaker, and crop needs.
6. Create platform-native crops or timeline trims while protecting active speakers, captions, and product/demo evidence.
7. Review the actual clip opening, context sufficiency, ending, caption-safe area, audio, and crop tracking.

When those capabilities are not supported, create a clipping plan and timeline markers from user-supplied timestamps/transcript, then state which transcription, ranking, crop, or render step remains external.

## Platform preset wrapper

A Shorts/Reels/TikTok request is a parameter preset over the clipping pipeline, not a separate intelligence layer. Set ratio, duration range, safe areas, and candidate count from the user's destination requirements, while retaining the same ranking, dedupe, and completion criteria.

## Done criteria for clipping

Return only surviving clips. Each must have a source range, a coherent hook-to-payoff arc, a reason for selection, the intended platform/ratio, and either a verified rendered artifact or an explicit pending render status.
