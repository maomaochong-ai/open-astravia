# Product, brand, and logo video recipes

## Animated 3D-style logo reveal

1. Start from a clean logo authority. Produce and approve a dimensional interpretation as a still before motion.
2. Define extrusion/depth, material, bevel/edge, background, and light behavior without changing the mark geometry.
3. Animate one reveal mechanism: controlled rotation, assembly, light sweep, or camera orbit. Keep readable logo hold time at the end.
4. Review spelling, silhouette, material continuity, aliasing, and whether the final mark can be read at delivery size.

Do not describe a raster interpretation as a true 3D asset. If editable geometry is required, hand off to a 3D/vector production step.

## Cinematic product ad

Use two cost gates:

1. Cheap exploration: 3-4 hero stills varying composition/light/material story while product invariants remain fixed.
2. User/quality selection: record the chosen hero and why.
3. Motion proof: animate a short representative reveal with one camera move and a defined final state.
4. Final production: upscale/rebuild the authority still if needed, render assigned shots, create audio separately or natively as supported, and return an ordered sequence manifest.

Useful shot jobs: world/need, reveal, material macro, use/benefit proof, and brand resolve. Do not ask one short shot to perform all five.

Fallback: if motion fails but the selected still is strong, deliver the approved hero and a still-led treatment with shot order and motion notes rather than replacing the product with an unstable video.

## Jewelry or small luxury object

Create one approved hero plus assigned macro views. Motion should be slow enough to preserve facets, settings, engravings, and specular behavior. Use a physically plausible camera/light plan such as a restrained orbit plus a separate macro glide. Reject warped settings, changing stones, crawling engravings, or highlight flicker.

## Ingredient or component burst

Approve a still where product, ingredients/components, and trajectories are individually readable. In motion, assign one action: separate, orbit, burst, or reassemble. State gravity/slow-motion behavior, camera stability, label visibility, and the resolved end state. Avoid simultaneous unexplained transformations.

## Giant product scene

The authority still must establish scale, ground contact, perspective, and human/environment relation. Animate with subtle subject behavior and a camera path that reinforces scale. Large fast product motion usually breaks geometry; prefer camera movement, environmental parallax, or small material effects.

## Premium product showcase from a source photo

1. Restage the product into a controlled hero environment while preserving geometry and labels.
2. Approve that still.
3. Add a single motion idea: slow push, orbit, surface-light sweep, or environmental micro-motion.
4. End on a clean product frame with enough hold for brand copy or an edit.

## Product campaign handoff

When the request also includes listing images, social crops, ad copy, or several shots, invoke `$create-content-campaign`. The selected hero becomes the asset authority for still derivatives and video keyframes; do not regenerate the product identity independently in each branch.
