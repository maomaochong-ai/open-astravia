# Camera, light, transition, and sound vocabulary

Use production terms only when they define an observable result.

## Framing and lens feel

- extreme wide/wide: geography and scale;
- medium/two-shot/OTS: body action and relationship;
- close/ECU: reaction and pressure;
- macro insert: material, object state, hand detail;
- POV/profile/silhouette/top-down/low/high/Dutch: viewpoint and power.

Lens shorthand: 24mm immersive space, 35mm documentary proximity, 50mm human intimacy, 85mm compression/portrait, 100mm macro detail, anamorphic wide. Also state the visible effect because numeric behavior varies by model.

## Camera movement

Static, push-in/pull-out, lateral tracking, follow, pan/tilt, orbit, crane/aerial, handheld, rack focus, whip pan, dolly zoom, FPV, speed ramp. Specify start, subject, direction/speed, foreground/background relationship, and rest point. Use one primary move per shot.

## Motivated light

Name source, direction, quality, color, affected surface, and narrative job:

- window/practical/phone/neon/fluorescent/headlight/fire as source;
- side/back/top/under/front/bounce/rim as direction;
- hard/soft/diffused/flickering/volumetric as quality;
- contact shadow, reflection, texture, and atmospheric response.

Define concrete palette roles instead of “cinematic colors.” One dominant source creates stronger hierarchy than many decorative lights.

## Transitions

Natural cut, fade, dissolve, flash, wipe, foreground occlusion, match cut, action/whip cut, motion relay, zoom-through, or stylized material transition. Name the trigger and what must align across the cut. One transition type per cut.

## Sound design

Plan ambience, body/action sound, punctual event, dialogue, music role, and silence. Sound may guide rhythm even when the selected model does not generate it. Keep audio intent separate from a claim that the adapter supports native audio.

Use sound as a causal anchor: fluorescent buzz stops at the decision, a click triggers a visual transition, breath replaces the engine at peak pressure. Dialogue gets a speaker, delivery, visible action, and timing budget.
