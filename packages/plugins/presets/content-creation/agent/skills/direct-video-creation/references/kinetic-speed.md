# Kinetic speed

Use for race, drift, chase, mechanical ritual, or other faceless speed sequences. Read `animatic-keyframes.md` first.

## Governing rules

- Speed is a spatial/physical fact, not a post-production adjective.
- Emotion lives in rubber, metal, controls, road, light, and sound when faces are absent.
- Choose one governing motif or visual promise for the piece.
- Tie panel/shot density to mechanical rhythm: idle holds, pressure shortens, launch bursts, aftermath holds.
- Every insert must mean setup, commitment, mistake, recovery, proof of speed, or result.

## Speed cues

A convincing still or shot combines several:

- camera at or below subject/bumper height;
- road/ground streaking through the lower frame;
- near foreground object passing an edge;
- sharp hero against a moving background;
- vibration/deformation cue: tire, suspension, mirror, gauge, fabric, spray.

Use a physically mountable viewpoint where realism matters. Alternate low-wide proximity with long-lens compression and distant proof shots so scale and closing distance remain readable.

## Mechanical story

Build an irreversible sequence such as:

`ritual/setup -> staging -> trigger -> launch/commitment -> contest/correction -> result -> release`

For drift: approach -> initiation -> counter-steer -> clipping point -> smoke/pressure -> transition -> exit. For a chase: geography -> threat entry -> narrowing options -> maneuver -> consequence -> new state.

## Object acting

- resolve: grip reaches maximum, pedal pins, control latches;
- strain: gauge reaches limit, material deforms, vibration increases;
- mistake: motion vector conflicts with intended path;
- recovery: control returns and state stabilizes;
- result: gap, line, residue, release, or stopped mechanism.

## Light and sound

Use one motivated practical per beat and let it rim material or atmosphere. Keep a disciplined base palette and reserve warm/danger colors for triggers or stress. Backlight smoke/spray so force becomes visible.

Sound defines the edit: mechanical idle, trigger silence, impact, contest layers, sudden intimate detail, final vacuum. Even without generated audio, plan the physical consequence each sound produces.

## Anti-fake gate

Reject clean god-angle action, stable cabin at speed, sharp wheels, beauty passes without force transmission, speed that only works after speeding up footage, impossible camera mounts, random ritual order, warm decorative wash, and any insert without a state-changing verb.
