# Image review rubric

## Must-pass gates

Any failure blocks approval:

- required subject, product, copy, or action is missing or wrong;
- delivery ratio/resolution or protected layout area is unusable;
- identity, logo, label, or product geometry violates a hard constraint;
- severe anatomy, perspective, object-merging, text, or crop artifact;
- output contradicts a required reference role.

## Scored dimensions

Score each 1-5 and cite visible evidence:

- Brief fidelity: objective and constraints are visibly satisfied.
- Composition: focal order, balance, crop, depth, and destination fit.
- Subject fidelity: identity, anatomy, geometry, materials, and interactions.
- Art direction: palette, lighting, texture, medium, and specificity cohere.
- Technical finish: edges, shadows, reflections, detail, text, and artifact control.
- Continuity: consistency with approved assets and the series ledger.

Do not use the total score to override a must-pass failure. Compare variants dimension by dimension; a useful winner may depend on whether fidelity, performance, or visual novelty is the current priority.
