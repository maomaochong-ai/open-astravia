# Scenario-specific quality gates

Apply the generic image/video rubric first, then the relevant gate below.

## Logo and brand system

- Exact brand spelling and stable mark geometry.
- Legible silhouette at small size and viable monochrome form.
- Variants derive from the selected mark rather than redesigning it.
- Palette/type labels are verified outside generated artwork.
- Mockups demonstrate use but do not masquerade as source assets.

## Ads, social covers, and thumbnails

- Message understood in roughly two seconds at destination size.
- One clear focal hierarchy; exact copy readable and within safe areas.
- Product/offer evidence supports the claim.
- Platform recomposition preserves intent without blind-crop damage.
- A/B variants change one named persuasion hypothesis.

## Ecommerce and product sets

- Authority geometry, color, material, label, controls, and scale persist.
- Each asset has an assigned listing/view job and adds new evidence.
- Main/listing compliance requirements are met.
- No invented feature, badge, review, certification, or comparison.
- Contact shadows, reflections, grips, and use physics are plausible.

## Identity, portrait, and try-on

- Face/body identity holds in every asset, not only the best one.
- Garment/product cut, pattern, logo, material, drape, and contact hold.
- Hands, occlusions, accessories, and body boundaries are coherent.
- Retouching does not erase identity or create plastic texture.
- Illustrative try-on/color advice is not presented as measured fact.

## Spatial and UI design

- Floor plan/room structure, openings, circulation, scale, and viewpoint are preserved where required.
- Furniture and components fit their physical/layout constraints.
- UI has a plausible user state, hierarchy, navigation, data, text fit, contrast, and controls.
- Generated visuals are labeled concepts, not construction documents or working software.

## Product/logo motion

- Product/mark remains recognizable through motion and in the final hold.
- Camera and light reveal form rather than causing geometry flicker.
- Macro details, labels, facets, or engravings do not crawl or mutate.
- Every shot has one job and a usable final state.

## UGC and character performance

- Hook, visible action, spoken line, and CTA fit the duration.
- Performance feels intentional and credible; product interaction is natural.
- Identity, hands, lip motion, product label, and audio sync survive over time.
- Claims are verified and generated content is not framed as authentic documentary evidence.

## Action, story, and tutorial

- Geography/process order can be followed without explanation.
- Identity, screen direction, props/tools, environment, and light remain continuous.
- Each timed beat contains one readable event and the cut density matches duration.
- Impact/process evidence uses an appropriate shot size.
- The final frame hands off cleanly to the next shot or resolves the sequence.

## Long-form clipping

- Clip opens on a self-contained hook and preserves enough setup for the payoff.
- Start/end do not cut words, reactions, demonstrations, or meaning.
- Ranked clips are distinct; low-value filler is not used to hit a count.
- Crop follows the active subject and protects captions/product evidence.
- Source range, rationale, destination format, and render status are recorded.
