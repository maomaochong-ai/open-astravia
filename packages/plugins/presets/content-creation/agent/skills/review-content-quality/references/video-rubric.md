# Video review rubric

## Inspect temporally

Review the opening state, first motion, middle, critical transformation/cut, final state, and audio alignment. A good still frame cannot prove good motion.

## Must-pass gates

- required subject/action/story beat and delivery format are present;
- identity, product, text, or continuity anchors do not fail materially;
- no severe popping, merging, deformation, disappearance, or incoherent transition;
- motion and camera remain watchable for the intended surface;
- unwanted speech, unsafe audio, or unusable end state is absent.

## Scored dimensions

Score each 1-5 with timestamped or phase-specific evidence:

- Brief fidelity: purpose, beats, constraints, and final state.
- Motion coherence: physical continuity, weight, speed, and causality.
- Camera control: framing, path, stability, focus, and relationship to action.
- Dramaturgy: trigger, response, progression, reveal, or hook.
- Visual continuity: identity, geometry, wardrobe, environment, light, and screen direction.
- Technical finish: temporal artifacts, detail stability, transitions, loop/cut readiness, and audio sync.

Do not hide a critical temporal failure inside an average score.
