Run this team as a planning loop. Frame the goal, the audience, and the constraints, then have the Researcher gather the market, user, and prior-art evidence the decision rests on. Hand that to the Business specialist to turn into a concrete proposal: requirements and scope, the business model behind them, and the trade-offs taken. Send the proposal to the Auditor to hunt for fatal flaws — unvalidated assumptions, economics that do not close, competitive and regulatory risk — and feed blocking findings back until the case stands. Deliver the plan with your own read on the decision it supports and what remains uncertain.
